Section 2.0 · Problem Pillars
§ 03 / 10
Administrative Bottlenecks

Three Critical Inefficiencies

Campus Queue Congestion

Fragmented departmental offices create massive physical congestion as thousands of students converge on desks simultaneously during exam weeks.

Deficiency Blindness

Students cannot see outstanding obligations (e.g. unreturned books, laboratory fees) beforehand. This results in wasted travel and administrative friction.

Vulnerable Audit Integrity

Paper cards lack immutable auditing. Forged signatures or undocumented overrides cannot be detected, violating government auditing rules.

Section 5.0 · Novelty
§ 06 / 10
Sourced Gap & Contrast

Core Research Novelty

Cryptographic Non-Repudiation

Existing school systems utilize standard relational databases where records are easily modified or back-dated by rogue DBAs. SwiftClear implements a cryptographically linked audit ledger.

Every status change is hashed with the previous record's hash, making silent unauthorized overrides mathematically impossible to hide.

Data Privacy & Minimization

Naively centralized campus portals often violate the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) by letting librarians or desk clerks view unrelated financial or academic student histories.

We enforce database Row-Level Security (RLS), isolating query bounds by role, ensuring desk heads only see clearance statuses corresponding to their own unit.

Section 6.0 · Boundaries
§ 07 / 10
Scope Limits

System Scope & Boundaries

Explicitly In Scope
  • Role-Based Portals: Independent dashboards for Library, Laboratory, Accounting, Dean, Registrar, and Student.
  • Relational Deficiency Logging: Administrators can create specific deficiencies linking description, fine, and status.
  • Asynchronous SMS Pipeline: Pushing status alerts using queue managers to cellular endpoints.
  • Hash-Chained Audit Logs: Immutable log tracking system modifications securely.
Explicitly Out of Scope
  • Automatic Payments: The system does *not* process direct credit cards or bank transfers. Fines are resolved manually when the student registers offline payment.
  • Scholastic GPA Processing: The system verifies holds logged by the registrar but does *not* compute transcript content or grade averages.
  • Automatic Biometrics: Identification relies on school email accounts and registered IDs rather than face/fingerprint scans.

Section 8.0 · Verification
§ 09 / 10
Falsifiable Benchmarks

Verification & Evaluation Plan

Automated Testing Metrics

  • Concurrency Test: JMeter scripts running 5,000 requests simulating 50 concurrent admin terminals. Target: zero lock deadlocks.
  • Network Latency Check: Measure delay between admin database write commit and socket message reception. Target: < 500ms.
  • SMS Dispatch Queue Latency: Benchmark delay from SMS job creation in Redis to gateway GSM transmission. Target: < 5 seconds.

User Evaluation Metrics

  • Queue Time Metric: Measure clearance transaction completion durations for 100 students. Benchmark target reduction: > 90%.
  • Usability validation: Deploy System Usability Scale (SUS) survey after campus trial run. Target score: > 80 (Grade A).
  • Data Minimization Validation: Audit administrative login sessions, ensuring zero cross-department data leaks are registered.
